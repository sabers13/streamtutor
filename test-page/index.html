How to test:
  1. Ensure the backend is running at http://localhost:8000.
  2. Load the unpacked extension directory (extension/dist) in chrome://extensions.
  3. Click Sentence 1 (B1): the German Stream Tutor overlay appears with translation, CEFR badge [B1], and grammar note.
  4. Click Sentence 2 (A1) or Sentence 4 (A2): the overlay automatically stays hidden because the default threshold is B1+ (A1/A2 are left untouched so learning is unobtrusive).
  5. To see assistance on A1 or A2 sentences as well, change the Help dropdown in the overlay to A2+ or A1+ (All)!
  6. Click Sentence 3 (B1): overlay appears with subordinate clause breakdown (*"obwohl"*).
  7. Mark Any Word (Yomitan-style): Click on any individual word in the German subtitle (e.g. wovon, überhaupt, schwach, redest). The word glows in amber, and a floating tooltip appears with the word definition and a [➕ Save Flashcard] button.
  8. 1-Click Save to File: Click [➕ Save Flashcard] or [💾 Sentence]. The card is instantly appended to backend/data/saved_flashcards.csv and ready to import into your Wortlaut app or Anki!
